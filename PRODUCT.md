# Otaku Oracle

An anime oracle for curious fans. Anyone can search any AniList title and ask a free-text question. The answer service routes fact, news, discussion and unclear questions deterministically. Checked claims carry source values and links; source agreement, disagreement, insufficient evidence and discussion remain distinct. AniList is the reference, not the truth.

Preserve the approved warm field-journal typography, real cover stack, synchronized source note, starter row and living dusk journey. The owner supplied a samurai atlas for the wandering companion. The identity uses its topknot/blade seal; no previous character art remains in the current product or fallback. The hero has quiet idle actions, suspended offscreen/hidden, with native scroll-distance walking and complete reduced-motion support.

No invented answers, source agreement, headlines or accuracy. Without the answer service the clearly labelled demo reads seven AniList fields and abstains on unmatched questions. With the source extension, two retrieved catalogs must agree, or an AniList-owned metric/graph/schedule is explicitly single-source. Missing, stale and unavailable evidence remains visible. Discussion links are not facts. Exact edition mapping is required; no title guessing.

The Why page reads runtime evaluation data. That preregistered evaluation measures AniList grounding only; cross-source comparison and discussion routing are unmeasured. Deployment remains a client plus separate answer Worker on the owner's existing account. No signup or deployment is part of this task; evaluation files and running processes are protected.
