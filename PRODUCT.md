# Otaku Oracle

An anime oracle for curious fans. Anyone can ask any question about any AniList title. The answer service drafts a response and compares tagged facts with its retrieved AniList record. Verified, conflict and unchecked states remain distinct. AniList is the reference, not the truth.

The owner explicitly rejected the muted atlas direction. Preserve the earlier field-journal warmth, italic serif type, real anime cover stacks, source note and cover starter row. Combine these with a cinematic moving dusk world and a large, expressive original mascot. Lead with anime, not auditing. Native scrolling must feel like camera travel, while keyboard and reduced-motion users retain a complete beautiful still page. Three solo critique/rebuild passes are required. No design interview is needed: full creative delegation is explicit.

No invented answers or accuracy. Without the answer service the clearly labelled demo reads seven source fields and abstains on unmatched questions. The Why page reads runtime evaluation data. The existing Worker contract stays unchanged. Deployment target: the current client with a separate answer Worker; no new account or paid service is required. No evaluation files may be changed.
