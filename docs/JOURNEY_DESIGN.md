# Journey direction

Solo design and review under the owner's explicit creative delegation. Product context comes from the supplied brief; no interview or external audience research is claimed.

Seven grounded directions considered: festival programme; rail-pass ledger; herbarium annotation; theatre scenery; illustrated walking atlas; film contact archive; cel-registration desk. The walking atlas is the fifth direction and the chosen form. Terminal-like alternatives lose product clarity and warmth; poster-only treatments lose the journey mechanism. No visual material was copied from the references.

## Direction contract
THESIS: curiosity becomes a route to a source. A continuous landscape travels sideways and diagonally rather than reading as a stack of promotional cards.
OWN-WORLD: warm paper, near-black forest, vermilion scarf/seal, pale sage. A flourished italic editorial voice meets precise plain interfaces. Original cut-paper scenery and an original traveller carry the space.
STORY: choose a title and question; see three ways a claim can land; follow the source instead of taking a confident sentence on trust.
FIRST VIEWPORT: a large left headline, working right-hand question desk, a winding source trail across the lower horizon and a little walking guide. Navigation and pause remain accessible.
FORM: illustrated walking atlas, direction five. The task form is available immediately and the subsequent scenes continue its route.
MOTION: native scroll moves one camera through a three-scene panorama; near and far layers travel at different speeds. The traveller reacts to input/loading/check/error; states use short mask/stamp transitions. Reduced motion and visible pause replace spatial movement with a readable static sequence.
